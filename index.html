An Imperial Odyssey

The Living Heritage of ZOHRA

Every weave at ZOHRA is an homage to six centuries of royal Indian textile tradition — resurrecting forgotten Mughal motifs with ethical weaver cooperatives.

Plate I — Master Weaver Mohammed Rafiq at the Pit Loom, Madanpura
Chapter I: The Sacred Warp

The Loom of Benares: Rhythms in Pure Silk

In the narrow lanes along the holy Ganges, our weavers practice the antique Kadwa and Fekwa techniques. Unlike modern automated Jacquards, each golden leaf motif is engraved by hand, taking up to three months to complete a single heirloom drape.

"We do not merely weave silk; we thread the memories of courtyards, monsoons, and Mughal palaces."
100% Silk Mark Certified
Fair-Wage Guild Guarantee
Chapter II: The Gilded Needle

The Thread of Gold: Pure Real Zari & Dabka

True Zari is not copper or synthetic plastic; it begins with silver ingots drawn into micro-filaments finer than a whisper, then electroplated with 24-karat gold leaf. Our master karigars in Awadh spend weeks couching this thread alongside basra seed pearls to forge regal court attire.

"When light cascades across true Zari, it does not flash — it glows with an eternal, sovereign warmth."
Hallmarked Pure Silver Core
Original Awadhi Stitches
